# The Builder and the Well

Juan David, this one is about you alone. No synastry, no one else's chart. Your birth chart, what it says about the life you have been living, and then a forecast for November two thousand twenty-six. Everything here was recalculated from the Swiss Ephemeris, degree by degree, for January tenth, nineteen ninety-one, eleven thirty-three in the morning, Bogotá. The symbols are old. The judgment about what to do with them is yours.

## The shape of the chart

Start with the shape before the details. Your chart has two centers of gravity. One is at the top, in the tenth house, where the Sun, Saturn and Neptune sit together in Capricorn, with Uranus a few degrees behind them. The other is deep in the eighth house, where the Moon and Pluto sit together in Scorpio. Everything else in the chart serves one of those two centers. The top is the builder: the public life, the work, the thing you will be known for. The bottom is the well: the private emotional life, the part of you that very few people are allowed to see.

Aries rising, at fifteen degrees. You enter rooms directly. People read you as decisive before you have said a word, and sometimes as more confrontational than you feel. The ruler of your chart is Mars, and your Mars is in Taurus, in the second house. That is the engine behind the Aries face: slow to start, hard to stop, and tied to the body, to money, and to what you own. You do not sprint. You push a heavy thing uphill for years and then it is at the top and nobody remembers it was ever anywhere else.

## The builder: the tenth house

The Sun at nineteen degrees of Capricorn in the tenth house is the clearest signature in the chart. Identity and vocation are the same thing for you. You do not have a job. You have a life's work, and you feel most like yourself when you are building it. That is not a flaw. It is also the thing that will make rest feel like failure if you let it.

Saturn sits seven degrees from the Sun, in its own sign. Sun with Saturn is the signature of the late bloomer and of the person who is held to a higher standard, first by a father figure or an authority, then by himself. It makes you reliable and it makes you hard on yourself. The gift is that your results compound. What you have at forty-five will be far larger than what you had at thirty, because you do not quit and you do not cut corners. The risk is that you treat your own worth as something that has to be earned again every morning.

Neptune sits five degrees on the other side of the Sun. This is less talked about and more important than it looks. Neptune with the Sun in the tenth gives vision. You can see a version of a project, a company, a city, that does not exist yet, and you can make other people see it too. It also blurs edges. Sometimes you commit to an image of a goal rather than the goal itself, and sometimes other people project onto you things you never claimed. The discipline for Neptune is simple and dull: write the plan down in numbers, then check the numbers against the dream once a quarter.

Uranus, a few degrees before the Midheaven, in the ninth house, adds the restlessness. You will not stay in a conventional track. Your career is likely to have breaks, pivots, something to do with other countries, with technology, or with ideas that were early. Mercury in Sagittarius in the ninth house agrees: your mind runs on big maps, travel, foreign ideas, teaching. You think better when you are explaining something to someone else, and you get bored fast with detail unless it serves a large picture.

Venus is also in the tenth house, in Aquarius, opposite Jupiter in Leo. Love and public life are linked for you. The people you fall for tend to be connected to your work, your circle, your projects, or they become part of them. Venus in Aquarius wants a partner who is first a friend and an equal, with her own mind and her own life. Jupiter opposite it, in the fifth house of romance and play, makes you generous and a little dramatic in love, and prone to wanting too much at once. The balance point is clear: friendship first, grand gestures second.

## The well: the eighth house

The Moon at twenty-two degrees of Scorpio, in the eighth house, two and a half degrees from Pluto. This is the most intense placement in your chart, and it is the one the people who only see the builder never meet.

You feel everything at full depth and you show almost none of it. You do not do casual intimacy. You decide, slowly and privately, who is allowed in, and once they are in, you are loyal to a fault. Betrayal is the one thing you do not recover from quickly, and you remember it longer than is useful. The Moon with Pluto also gives you an instinct for what is hidden: in people, in deals, in rooms. You read motives well. The shadow side is control. When you feel unsafe, the reflex is to manage the situation, the person, the information. The work of a lifetime with this Moon is to learn that you can be safe without being in control.

The eighth house is also the house of other people's resources, inheritance, shared money, and transformation through crisis. The lesson tends to arrive through a few deep losses or endings, each of which makes you a stronger and quieter person. By thirty-five you already know what that feels like.

The Moon is opposite your Mars, six degrees. Emotion and action pull in different directions. When you are hurt you want to act, build, fix, push. When you push, the feeling goes underground and comes back later, bigger. The remedy is boring: when something lands hard, wait forty-eight hours before deciding anything.

The Moon is in an easy sextile to your Sun. The builder and the well are not at war. Your emotional depth feeds your work, and your work gives your feelings somewhere to go. That is the healthiest line in the chart.

## The body and the money: Mars in the second house

Mars in Taurus in the second house, in a close trine to Saturn. This is the steadiest money signature a chart can have. You earn through persistence and craft, not through gambles. Your income grows in steps, with long plateaus. You are not built for speculation and it will not reward you. You are built for owning things: skills, property, equity you have earned over time.

The same Mars governs the body. Taurus Mars is strong, enduring, and slow to warm up. Your physical health depends on rhythm more than intensity: regular sleep, regular meals, regular training, and weight on the bar rather than sprints. The throat and neck, the Taurus zone, are where tension shows first. When you are stressed you will clench your jaw before you notice anything else. That is your early warning system. Use it.

## The creative fifth house

Jupiter in Leo in the fifth house, trine your Ascendant. Children, creativity, teaching, play, romance: this is where your luck lives. It is also the part of the chart you most neglect when the tenth house takes over. When life feels gray, the fix is not more work. It is making something, teaching someone, or playing with a child. Jupiter here also suggests you would be a warm, generous father, and that fatherhood, if you choose it, would be one of the great expansions of your life rather than a limit on it.

## The long cycles so far

A few dates, so you can check the chart against your own memory.

Around two thousand seventeen to two thousand nineteen, Pluto crossed your Sun. That was a period of identity being taken apart and rebuilt, usually through work or through authority: a job, a company, a path that ended or changed shape, and a harder, clearer you on the other side.

In early two thousand twenty, Saturn returned to its own place at twenty-seven degrees of Capricorn. Your Saturn return. Whatever you committed to then, or whatever commitment ended then, set the frame for this decade.

Through two thousand twenty-three and twenty-four, Uranus in Taurus opposed your Moon. Something in your private emotional life was shaken loose, often a relationship or a living situation that had become too fixed. It felt like disruption. It was decompression.

Now, in two thousand twenty-six, you are in a twelfth house profection year, which runs until your birthday in January. The twelfth house is the house of endings, retreat, rest, and unfinished business. It is the last year of a twelve year cycle. Saturn and Neptune are both sitting in that house right now, in early Aries, moving toward your Ascendant, which they will cross in two thousand twenty-seven. Read that plainly: this is the year before a new beginning, not the beginning itself. The task is to close things, rest properly, and get honest about what comes with you into the next cycle and what does not. On your birthday the profection moves to the first house, ruled by Mars, and the engine restarts.

And in the background, Pluto in early Aquarius is moving toward your Venus in the tenth house. That contact becomes exact in two thousand twenty-seven and twenty-eight. Your values, your relationships, and your public role will be transformed together. What you want from love and what you want from work will stop being separate questions. November is early for that. But you will feel it starting.

## November two thousand twenty-six

Here is the month, in four windows. The planets set the weather. You still choose what to do in it.

### First window: November first to the thirteenth

Mercury is retrograde in Scorpio in your seventh house of partners until the thirteenth. Venus is retrograde in Libra in the same house until the fourteenth. Both are moving backward through the part of your chart that governs relationships and agreements. The instruction is the same one from the synastry episode: revise, observe, listen. Do not sign, declare, or renegotiate anything important in this window if it can wait two weeks. Old conversations and old people may resurface. Let them. You do not have to answer in the moment.

Mars is in Leo in your fifth house all month, and it squares your natal Pluto on the third and your natal Moon on the eighth. Those are the two short-fuse days. The feeling will be that someone is trying to control you or that you must control something. Neither is usually true. On those days, move your body hard in the morning and do not have the difficult conversation after dark.

The New Moon is on November ninth, at seventeen degrees of Scorpio, sitting almost exactly on the cusp of your eighth house. This is the most personal lunation of the year for you. It is a seed for intimacy, for shared resources, for trust. Set one quiet intention about what you want to let in. Do not announce it.

### Second window: November twelfth to the fifteenth

Three days that work like a private new year. The Sun crosses your natal Pluto on the twelfth and your natal Moon on the fourteenth. Mercury turns direct on the thirteenth. Venus turns direct on the fourteenth. The deepest part of you is lit up while the relationship planets stop and turn forward. This is a window for honesty with yourself. Write. Walk alone. Say the true thing in a journal before you say it to anyone else. If you have been avoiding a feeling about the connection we discussed in the other episode, this is when it surfaces. Let it.

### Third window: November sixteenth to the twenty-fourth

Now things move forward. Jupiter in Leo is in a tight trine to your natal Mercury all month, exact on the twenty-ninth. Ideas land, messages get answered, travel and learning open up, and your words carry more weight than usual. Use it for pitches, proposals, teaching, writing. This is the most productive stretch of the month for the builder.

Two days to manage physically. On the twentieth, the transiting Sun opposes your natal Mars. On the twenty-second, transiting Mars squares your natal Mars. Energy runs high and a little reckless. These are not days for maximum lifts, long drives when tired, or arguments you do not need. Train at eighty percent. Sleep. The strength is still there on the twenty-third.

The Full Moon is on November twenty-fourth, at three degrees of Gemini, in your second house, with Uranus nearby. This is a culmination around money and resources: a payment, a decision about income, a contract, or an unexpected change in what you thought was fixed. Full Moons reveal. Whatever surfaces about your finances in that week is information you needed. Do not react in the first forty-eight hours. Decide in the first week of December.

### Fourth window: November twenty-fifth to the thirtieth

Venus, now direct, makes an exact sextile to your natal Mercury on the twenty-eighth. This is the window the synastry episode pointed to: conversations flow, affection is easy to express in words, and a message you send is likely to be received well. If there is something warm to say to someone, say it on the twenty-seventh or twenty-eighth.

On the twenty-ninth, Venus squares your natal Saturn. A reality check follows the warmth. A delay, a limit, a "not yet," or simply the recognition that something real takes time. This is not a rejection. It is Saturn asking whether you are willing to go slowly. For your chart, the honest answer has always been yes.

On the twenty-ninth and thirtieth, Mercury, moving forward again, crosses your natal Pluto and then your natal Moon as the month ends. A deep conversation closes the month. It may be with someone else or it may be with yourself. Either way, something that was underground gets words.

## The one thing to remember

November is a month of turning. The first half asks you to stop and look back. The second half lets you move. The forecast cannot tell you what will happen. It can tell you where the pressure is and where the openings are. The pressure is on control, in the first week and around the twenty-second. The openings are on honesty, around the thirteenth, and on words, around the twenty-eighth.

You are the builder and the well. The builder knows how to wait. The well knows how to feel. This month, let the well speak first and the builder answer second. Then rest, because the new cycle starts in January, and you will want to be ready.
